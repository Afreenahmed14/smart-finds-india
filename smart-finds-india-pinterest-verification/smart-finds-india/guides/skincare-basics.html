SKINCARE

A Beginner's Guide to Building a Simple Skincare Routine

A gentle, no-fuss framework for understanding cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen—and introducing products carefully.

SMART FINDS INDIA • PRACTICAL BUYING GUIDE

The simple three-step foundation

**1. Cleanse.** Choose a cleanser that removes everyday dirt, sweat and sunscreen without leaving your skin feeling uncomfortably tight. Some people prefer a gentle rinse in the morning and a more thorough cleanse in the evening.

How to choose products

Start with your main concern: dryness, oiliness, sensitivity or simply maintaining a comfortable routine. Read the ingredient list and usage instructions rather than relying only on front-label claims. Fragrance-free products may be worth considering if fragrance tends to bother your skin, but no single formula suits everyone.

Introduce one new product at a time

Adding several new products together makes it difficult to identify what caused irritation. Patch-test a new product according to its instructions, introduce it gradually and stop using it if you develop a reaction. Persistent irritation, painful acne, rashes or other skin concerns are reasons to consult a dermatologist.

Ingredients: keep the claims in perspective

Ingredients such as niacinamide, salicylic acid, retinoids and vitamin C are used in different types of skincare products, but their suitability depends on the person, formulation and how they are used. More active ingredients do not automatically mean better results. Follow product directions and seek professional advice if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, using prescription treatments or managing a skin condition.

A sensible starter shopping list

- A gentle cleanser - A moisturiser that feels comfortable - Broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higher - Optional: one targeted product for a clearly identified concern

The smart-buy takeaway

Prioritise products you will use regularly, clear ingredient and usage information, suitable packaging and a price you can sustain. Avoid buying a full routine because of a trend or a dramatic before-and-after image. This is general information, not personalised medical advice.

How to build a simple starter basket

Choose one cleanser, one moisturiser and one broad-spectrum sunscreen that suits your needs. You do not need to buy every item in these tables. A product's low price or popularity does not establish that it is suitable for your skin. Introduce new products one at a time and stop using a product if it causes irritation. For persistent skin concerns, ask a dermatologist.
