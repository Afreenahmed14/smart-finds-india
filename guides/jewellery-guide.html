A Practical Guide to Buying Everyday Jewellery

Compare jewellery materials, plating, closures, sizing and care—and learn what product descriptions should clarify.

SMART FINDS INDIA • PRACTICAL BUYING GUIDE

Understand the material description

Jewellery listings may describe pieces as solid gold, gold-plated, gold-tone, sterling silver, stainless steel, alloy or imitation jewellery. These terms are not interchangeable. Read the full material description and ask the seller for clarification if it is vague. A gold-tone finish, for example, describes appearance and does not by itself establish gold content.

Check plating and care

Plated finishes can wear with use, friction, moisture and exposure to cosmetics. Follow the care instructions and remove pieces when advised. Avoid assuming that a piece is waterproof or suitable for continuous wear unless the product information specifically supports that claim.

Get the size right

For rings, use a reliable sizing guide and check the seller's measurement method. For necklaces, compare the stated chain length with a necklace you already own. For bracelets, check both length and adjustability. Earring dimensions and weight can also affect comfort.

Inspect closures and construction

Look for clear photographs of clasps, posts, hooks, links and settings. Check that stones or decorative parts appear securely fitted. Read reviews for repeated comments about broken clasps, discolouration or missing pieces, while remembering that individual experiences can vary.

Consider sensitivities

If you have a known metal allergy or sensitivity, do not rely on vague terms such as “skin-friendly”. Check the exact metal composition and seek clarification from the seller. If the composition is unknown, consider choosing a product with clearer material information.

Everyday jewellery checklist

- Exact base material and finish - Dimensions, length and weight - Closure type and adjustability - Care and storage instructions - Stone details, if applicable - Seller information and return policy

The smart-buy takeaway

Choose pieces with transparent material descriptions and dimensions. Store jewellery dry and separately where appropriate, follow care instructions, and avoid paying a premium for claims that the listing does not substantiate.
